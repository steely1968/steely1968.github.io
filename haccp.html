Level 2 HACCP Course: Fundamentals, Responsibilities, and Compliance

Course Overview

This Level 2 HACCP (Hazard Analysis and Critical Control Points) training provides food handlers, supervisors, managers, and Food Business Operators (FBOs) with a clear understanding of food safety management systems. The curriculum breaks down each stage of the HACCP framework, offering practical guidance on how to maintain safety and efficiency in daily operations.

While FBOs hold the ultimate legal responsibility for monitoring food safety compliance, those tasked with developing, implementing, or maintaining a custom HACCP system must possess verified training in its application. Consequently, FBOs and individuals directly managing these safety protocols are strongly encouraged to progress to the Level 3 HACCP course.
